# Our approach: a custom portal beyond monitoring

Many solar businesses treat their website as a brochure: a WordPress or Joomla
template, stock images, a contact form. Not a place where customers actually
interact with the business. In this document we explain why we think that is worth
changing, and why we put in the effort to make a portal of your own as easy as
possible to have.

## The customer relationship starts before monitoring

Your interaction with a customer rarely begins the moment their system comes online.
It starts earlier: a quote, a site visit, a product purchase, an installation. By
the time monitoring is relevant, you already have a relationship with that customer
and data about them.

Solar monitoring is the most visited touchpoint your customer has with your brand,
but it reflects only part of the relationship. Your own portal is a great
place for a holistic view of it.

## A simple login today, a foundation for tomorrow

Many businesses don't initially see the need for a user login on their website,
but it is one of the most future-proof things you can add. A portal gives your
customers a single place to see their full history with your business: what they
purchased, who their installer is, their service contacts, and more. It also opens
a natural channel for upsells: extended warranties, upgrades, maintenance plans,
presented in the context of what they already own.

That information is different for every business, and it belongs with you,
alongside your branding, your pricing, and your customer records. This is why
SolarAssistant does not try to build it for you. We stay focused on monitoring;
you own the customer experience. When a user views their profile or a site detail,
we link through to your portal rather than showing a generic record inside the
monitoring app.

## AI is here

With tools like Claude or ChatGPT, making the portal your own comes down to a few prompts. Start from
a template, point the tool at it and at this repository, describe what you want, and it handles the
implementation. What used to require a developer and days of work is now accessible to any business
willing to spend an afternoon on it.

## What this means in practice

When a user registers a device or views a site, SolarAssistant links to your
domain rather than back to solar-assistant.io. Your portal handles the rest:
the profile, the site detail, the history, and whatever else is relevant to your
business. This repository gives you the building blocks to make that work.
